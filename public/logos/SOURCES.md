# Channel logo sources

All files come from Wikimedia Commons, where each one is tagged `PD-textlogo` (public domain because it is below the threshold of originality) and `Trademarked`. They remain trademarks of their owners and are used here only to identify each channel in a historical archive, with no endorsement implied.
